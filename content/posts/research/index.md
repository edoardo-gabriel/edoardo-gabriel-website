## Ongoing Projects

### Sunlight and Urban Crime

I study how natural light affects crime in Chicago. I build a street-segment measure of sunlight, using LiDAR data on buildings and the sun's position, and use it to compare streets that gain different amounts of light after the spring Daylight Saving Time transition. This isolates the effect of light from the other ways DST changes behavior, such as sleep, temperature, and daily routines.

### How Agricultural Subsidies Shape the Market for Animal Welfare

I study how agricultural subsidies affect the disconnect between consumers' stated support for animal welfare and their revealed preferences in purchasing decisions, using evidence from Germany's animal welfare labeling policy.
